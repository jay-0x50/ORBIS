# 얼굴·머리카락 텍스처 생성 프롬프트

도구: 내장 imagegen. 외부 CLI/API 키는 사용하지 않았다. 아래 프롬프트에서 2048²를 요청했고, 도구가 반환한 원본은 실제 1254×1254 PNG다. 원본 픽셀을 유지하고 Unity 임포트 최대 크기 2048, NPOT 유지, sRGB로 연결한다.

참조: 사용자가 제공한 Assets/Img/여주인공.png 및 남주인공.png. 생성 결과는 얼굴의 UV 알베도와 헤어 알베도에 사용하며 완성 모델 렌더 이미지와 구분한다.

NO hair, NO ears, NO head outline or oval boundary, NO neck, NO clothing, NO jewelry, NO background objects, NO labels/text, NO cast shadows, NO lighting vignette. Paint only polished frontal facial features. Exact layout measured from LEFT/TOP of square: eye centers (30%,40%) and (70%,40%); eyebrows centered at y=28%; very small nose tip (50%,58%); closed mouth (50%,75%) with width 17%. Direct forward symmetrical orthographic view, aligned forward gaze, no rotation or perspective. Upper18%, bottom10%, outer9% margins are continuous pale skin for safe UV blending. Neutral unlit albedo, soft hand-painted gradients, fine refined anime RPG linework, no photographic pores, no heavy outline. Output only the actual game texture. Subject: Stella, the beautiful elegant young ADULT heroine in the reference. Large clear icy blue/teal irises with deep-blue upper gradient, subtle radial details and delicate natural catchlights. Each almond eye 26% canvas width and11% height. Soft pink inner eye corners, tapered dark-umber upper eyelashes with fine outward flicks, very fine lower lash line, soft ash-blond thin eyebrows. Tiny understated nose with almost no nostril line, faint warm pink blush, small restrained rose-peach lips in a gentle subtle smile. No bulging round doll eyes, no large nose or wide grin, no child or chibi proportions.
